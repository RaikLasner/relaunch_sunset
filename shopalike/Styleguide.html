1. Color palette

Derived from the existing Ladenzeile/Shopalike assets: the logo blue #0A00DA is the primary color, and a soft paper tone #F7F3EE from the current navbar and footer is the brand surface. Each color has exactly one job: blue is reserved for the brand and actions, prices are ink, free shipping is a muted green, sale is a muted red. The product photos supply the color. The UI stays calm and neutral.

2. Typography

Taken from the current Ladenzeile pages. Canela Deck (medium) is used for headlines and gives the brand its editorial, fashion-magazine tone. Segoe UI is used for interface and body text. Canela is a licensed font. In the prototype, Newsreader (Google Fonts) is the fallback, and Inter stands in for Segoe UI on non-Windows systems.

3. Buttons

All buttons are pill-shaped (border-radius: 999px). The primary button is Ladenzeile blue, and its hover darkens to #0800a8. The outline button is used for secondary actions such as "Zum Shop" in the showcases and in the offer list.

The clickdummy covers three main templates. All of them share the same cream header, category menu (10 full-inventory categories) and footer.

Hero with search, trust bar, mixed brand and retailer logo carousels, category showcases (fashion, accessories, living, electronics), most searched items and USP blocks.
